# Team photos

Drop a photo here named after the person, then add the filename to their entry
in `src/data/site.js` as `photo: '/photos/team/elena-novikova.jpg'`.

Until a photo is set, the card falls back to an initials monogram, so the site
never shows a broken image or an empty frame.

Recommended: square crop, 800x800 or larger, shot against a plain or softly
blurred background so the four cards sit together as a set.
